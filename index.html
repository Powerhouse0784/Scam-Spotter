How the verdict works

Each tactic found is a signal with its own weight. Signals are combined so that several independent red flags compound, while a single weak one never tips a message into “scam”.

  • Language — urgency, threats, secrecy, prizes, fee demands, credential requests.
  • Links — IP hosts, shorteners, punycode, look-alike brand domains, risky endings, credentials-in-URL.
  • Sender — brand look-alikes, free-mail impersonation, banks texting from mobile numbers.

Know the playbook.

Six scams behind most losses in India. Learn the shape and the message stops working on you.

01
📞

Digital arrest

A caller in “police” or “CBI” uniform on video says you’re linked to a crime or parcel.

Spot it
Video-call officers, orders to stay on the line, a “verification” transfer.

Do this
No agency arrests by video call. Hang up and call 1930.

02
🔗

KYC & SIM-block SMS

A text says your account or SIM will be blocked today unless you update details.

Spot it
A link plus a deadline, from a mobile number or odd domain.

Do this
Never tap it. Open the official app or type the bank’s address yourself.

03
💼

Fake jobs & task scams

Easy pay for likes, reviews or “tasks”, usually on Telegram or WhatsApp.

Spot it
Pay-to-start fees, early small payouts, then bigger “deposits”.

Do this
Real employers never charge to hire you. Stop at the first fee.

04
📲

UPI collect & QR tricks

Someone “sends” money by asking you to scan a QR or approve a request.

Spot it
You’re asked for your UPI PIN to “receive” money.

Do this
You never enter a PIN to receive money. Decline the request.

05
📦

Courier & customs fees

A parcel is “held” until you pay a small clearance fee.

Spot it
A parcel you didn’t expect, a payment link, a courier lookalike.

Do this
Track via the courier’s official site. Don’t pay through links.

06
📈

Investment & crypto groups

A group admin shares profit screenshots and guaranteed returns.

Spot it
Guaranteed profit, pressure to top up, withdrawal “taxes”.

Do this
Verify SEBI registration. No honest investment guarantees returns.

Test yourself

Questions, answered.

Is my message uploaded?

The rule engine runs entirely in your browser. Only if you press the Gemini button, ask a follow-up or upload a screenshot is the content sent to our server function, which forwards it to Google’s Gemini API. We don’t store it. Gemini only receives your message when you explicitly click "Gemini second opinion" or ask a follow-up question.

How accurate is it?

The rule engine catches well-known scam patterns and explains each one. It can’t guarantee a message is genuine, so when money, logins or codes are involved, always verify through the official app or website. A low score is not a guarantee of safety.

I already paid or shared an OTP. Now what?

Call your bank immediately to block the card or account, then call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. The follow-up chat after a Gemini opinion can walk you through the steps.

Which languages does it understand?

The built-in detectors read English wording. Gemini handles other languages, and can explain its findings in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu or Marathi.

Is it free?

Yes, the core scanner is free. See the plans above for Family Shield and campus licences.

Privacy & limitations

Your data

The rule engine runs entirely in your browser. Your messages are never sent to our servers unless you explicitly choose to use a Gemini feature (second opinion, follow-up chat, or screenshot OCR). Even then, we do not store your messages; they are forwarded to Google's Gemini API for processing and then discarded.

Gemini's role

Gemini is an optional second opinion. It does not change the rule-based risk score. When you click "Gemini second opinion," your message text is sent to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API. The same applies to screenshot OCR and follow-up questions.

Limitations

ScamSpotter is a rule-based detector. It catches known patterns but cannot guarantee that a message is genuine. A low score means "no known scam patterns found," not "safe." Always verify through official channels when money, logins or personal information are involved.

Scan history

Scan history is off by default. You can opt in to save your last 20 scans on this device. No data is sent to our servers for storage.
