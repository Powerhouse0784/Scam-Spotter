How the verdict works

Each tactic found is a signal with its own weight. Signals are combined so that several independent red flags compound, while a single weak one never tips a message into “scam”.

  • Language — urgency, threats, secrecy, prizes, fee demands, credential requests.
  • Links — IP hosts, shorteners, punycode, look-alike brand domains, risky endings, credentials-in-URL.
  • Sender — brand look-alikes, free-mail impersonation, banks texting from mobile numbers.

Know the playbook.

Six scams behind most losses in India. Learn the shape and the message stops working on you.

01

Digital arrest

A caller in “police” or “CBI” uniform on video says you’re linked to a crime or parcel.

Spot it
Video-call officers, orders to stay on the line, a “verification” transfer.

Do this
No agency arrests by video call. Hang up and call 1930.

02

KYC & SIM-block SMS

A text says your account or SIM will be blocked today unless you update details.

Spot it
A link plus a deadline, from a mobile number or odd domain.

Do this
Never tap it. Open the official app or type the bank’s address yourself.

03

Fake jobs & task scams

Easy pay for likes, reviews or “tasks”, usually on Telegram or WhatsApp.

Spot it
Pay-to-start fees, early small payouts, then bigger “deposits”.

Do this
Real employers never charge to hire you. Stop at the first fee.

04

UPI collect & QR tricks

Someone “sends” money by asking you to scan a QR or approve a request.

Spot it
You’re asked for your UPI PIN to “receive” money.

Do this
You never enter a PIN to receive money. Decline the request.

05

Courier & customs fees

A parcel is “held” until you pay a small clearance fee.

Spot it
A parcel you didn’t expect, a payment link, a courier lookalike.

Do this
Track via the courier’s official site. Don’t pay through links.

06

Investment & crypto groups

A group admin shares profit screenshots and guaranteed returns.

Spot it
Guaranteed profit, pressure to top up, withdrawal “taxes”.

Do this
Verify SEBI registration. No honest investment guarantees returns.
