Know the playbook

Six scams cause a large share of online losses in India. Learn their shape and the message stops working on you.

01
📞

Digital arrest

A caller claiming to be police or CBI says you are linked to a crime or parcel.

Spot it: Video-call “officers”, orders to stay on the line, or a “verification” transfer.

Do this: No agency arrests people by video call. Hang up and call 1930.

02
🔗

KYC & SIM-block SMS

A text says your account or SIM will be blocked unless you update details immediately.

Spot it: A link plus a deadline, often from a mobile number or odd domain.

Do this: Never tap the link. Open the official app or type the bank’s address yourself.

03
💼

Fake jobs & tasks

Easy pay for likes, reviews, or “tasks”, usually through WhatsApp or Telegram.

Spot it: Registration fees, early small payouts, then requests for larger deposits.

Do this: Real employers do not charge you to get hired.

04
📲

UPI collect & QR tricks

Someone asks you to scan a QR code or approve a request to “receive” money.

Spot it: You are asked for your UPI PIN to receive money.

Do this: You never enter a PIN to receive money. Decline the request.

05
📦

Courier & customs fees

A parcel is supposedly held until you pay a small clearance fee.

Spot it: An unexpected parcel, a payment link, or a courier lookalike.

Do this: Track parcels only through the courier’s official website or app.

06
📈

Investment & crypto groups

A group admin shares profit screenshots and promises guaranteed returns.

Spot it: Guaranteed profit, pressure to top up, or withdrawal “taxes”.

Do this: Verify SEBI registration. No honest investment guarantees returns.

Questions, answered

Is my message uploaded?

No. This version analyzes messages entirely in your browser. Scan history is saved locally only if you opt in.

How accurate is it?

It detects common scam patterns and explains them. It cannot guarantee that a message is genuine. When money, logins, or codes are involved, verify through an official app or website.

I already paid or shared an OTP. What now?

Call your bank immediately to block the card or account. Then call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

Which languages does it understand?

The built-in detectors understand common English, Hindi, and Hinglish scam wording.

Is it free?

Yes. The core scanner is free. Family and campus plans are marked as planned.
